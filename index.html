Nutrition

Fuel Your Performance

Expert-backed nutrition guidance to complement your training and accelerate results.

Prioritize Protein

Aim for 1.6-2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily. Distribute intake evenly across meals to maximize muscle protein synthesis.

Hydrate Strategically

Drink at least 3 liters of water daily. Add electrolytes during intense training sessions to maintain peak performance.

Time Your Carbs

Consume complex carbohydrates 2-3 hours before training. Post-workout, pair fast-digesting carbs with protein within 45 minutes.

Sleep for Recovery

Quality sleep (7-9 hours) is when your body repairs muscle tissue. Avoid heavy meals and screens 60 minutes before bed.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes FitFlow different from other gyms?

FitFlow is a premium fitness experience, not just a gym. We combine elite-certified coaching, science-backed programming, luxury facilities, and a comprehensive mobile app to deliver a complete wellness ecosystem. Our 98% client satisfaction rate and personalized approach set us apart from traditional fitness centers.

Can I try before committing to a membership?

Absolutely. We offer a complimentary 3-day pass that gives you full access to our facilities, two group classes, and a consultation with one of our senior coaches. This allows you to experience the FitFlow difference firsthand before making any commitment.

Are the trainers certified?

Yes, every FitFlow trainer holds nationally recognized certifications (NASM, ACE, ISSA, or equivalent) plus specialized credentials in their area of expertise. Many hold advanced degrees in exercise science, kinesiology, or nutrition. We invest heavily in continuing education for our entire team.

Is there a cancellation fee?

We believe in earning your loyalty, not locking you in. All memberships can be canceled with 30 days' notice. There are no hidden cancellation fees or long-term contracts. If you're on an annual plan and cancel early, we simply prorate the remaining time.

Do you offer nutrition guidance?

Yes. Pro members receive a nutrition plan review, and Elite members receive fully customized meal plans updated monthly. All members have access to our library of nutrition resources, recipes, and guidance through the FitFlow app.
